After the restructure

A management simulation

Your company has just gone through a major restructure. Roles have changed, teams have been reshuffled, and morale is uncertain.

Over 8 weeks, you'll manage four employees through the transition. Each week you'll face a situation with every team member — your choices shape their engagement.

Three metrics track each person: office attendance, message response speed, and deadline completion. Let someone slide into crisis and the options available to you will change.

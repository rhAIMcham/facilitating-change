After the restructure

A management simulation

Your company has just gone through a major restructure. Roles have changed, teams have been reshuffled, and morale is uncertain.

You are the manager of a small team who handles escalated customer complaints. Over 4 weeks, you'll manage four employees through the transition. Two employees were already in your team, and two employees have been transferred into your team. Click on their character profiles to learn more about them.

Each week you'll face a situation with every team member — your choices shape their engagement. Understand their goals and needs to best guide them through this time.

Three metrics track each person: office attendance, message response speed, and deadline completion. Let someone slide into crisis and the options available to you will change.

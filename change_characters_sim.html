After the restructure

A management simulation

Your company has just undergone a major restructure Roles have changed, teams have been reorganised, and current morale in the workplace is low.

You are the manager of a small team that handles escalated customer complaints. Over 4 weeks, you’ll manage four employees through this transition. Two employees were already in your team, and two employees have been transferred from other departments. Click on their character profiles to learn more about them.

Each week you must navigate a situation with every team member– your choices will affect their engagement at work. Understanding their goals and needs are crucial to guiding them successfully in this uncertain time.

Three metrics track each employee’s mood: office attendance, message response speed, and deadline completion. Let someone escalate into crisis and the options available to you will change.

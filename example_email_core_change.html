Hi team,

I’m writing to share a clear update on the organisational change currently underway and what it means for us in Fundraising.

Why now (reason + benefits):

Over the past year, we’ve seen donor expectations shift and our program demand increase, while our internal processes have become harder to navigate across teams. As a not-for-profit, every hour we spend on duplicated effort or unclear handoffs is an hour we’re not spending engaging supporters and funding the work our communities rely on. This change is happening now so we can simplify how we work, strengthen stewardship and make it easier for donors to give and stay connected.

What’s changing (the change):

From 1 April, fundraising will move to a model that groups our work into three clear streams: Community Events, Major Gifts and Regular Giving/Appeals. For our team, that means:

  • Event planning and delivery will sit within the Community Events stream, with clearer coordination support from Marketing and Programs.
  • Donor follow-up and stewardship will be coordinated through a single shared donor journey, so supporters receive consistent communications regardless of the event they attend.
  • We’ll use one standard event timeline and reporting process across all regions to reduce last-minute work and improve visibility.

What’s staying the same:

Our purpose and priorities are unchanged: we’re still here to mobilise supporters, raise funds ethically, and connect donors to the impact they make possible. Your roles remain focused on organising and delivering fundraising events - there are no redundancies as part of this change. We’ll also keep the local relationships you’ve built; what’s changing is how we coordinate and support those relationships across the organisation.

What it means for you (what it means for me):

In the short term, you can expect some adjustments to how we plan events and share information - especially around timelines, approvals and reporting. You’ll have clearer points of contact for marketing assets, program stories and donor stewardship, and you’ll spend less time chasing updates or recreating materials. We’ll provide training and templates, and we’ll run two drop-in sessions so you can ask questions and raise risks early.

What happens next (process + next steps):

  • This Friday: I’ll share a one-page overview of the new streams, key contacts and the standard event timeline.
  • Next week: We’ll hold a team meeting to walk through the changes and capture questions for an updated FAQ.
  • Following two weeks: Training on the new event timeline, reporting and the shared donor journey.
  • Ongoing: Weekly 15-minute check-ins for “what we know / what we’re confirming / next steps” until the transition settles.

I know organisational change can feel unsettling, even when it’s aimed at making things better. If you’re unsure how this will affect your current events, your workload or your stakeholders, please reply to this email or book time with me - your questions are welcome, and I’ll be direct about what we know now and what we’re still confirming.

Thank you for the work you do and the care you bring to our supporters and mission. This change is about helping you do that work with more clarity, support, and impact.
